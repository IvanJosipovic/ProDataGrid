# Flat-layout benchmark results — 2026-08-11

These results compare the opt-in flat sibling surface with the nested
compatibility layout on the same 149,792-node hierarchy and the same 1200 × 760
headless host. The DataGrid viewport is 520 pixels high. Every value comes from
BenchmarkDotNet 0.15.8 in a Release build with tiered compilation disabled.

Environment: macOS 26.6 (Darwin 25.6.0), Apple M3 Pro (11 cores), .NET SDK
10.0.201, .NET 10.0.5 Arm64 RyuJIT, concurrent workstation GC. Each full-suite
case used three launches, three warmups, and ten measured iterations. The OS did
not permit BenchmarkDotNet to raise process priority.

`Ratio` and `RatioSD` are BenchmarkDotNet's distributions of flat time divided
by nested time. A ratio below 1 is a flat speedup; a ratio above 1 is a flat
slowdown. `Allocated` is managed allocation traffic per operation. It is not
retained heap, RSS, native, or GPU memory.

## Pending layout after collapse

Iteration setup expands and lays out the hierarchy, then dispatches
`CollapseAll`. The measured operation completes the pending Avalonia layout.

| Cell path | Nested mean | Flat mean | BDN ratio ± SD | Flat result | Nested allocated | Flat allocated | Allocation ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard retained | 3.208 ms | 3.511 ms | 1.10 ± 0.07 | 10% slower | 1117.59 KB | 1187.95 KB | 1.06 |
| Optimized retained theme | 2.764 ms | 2.949 ms | 1.07 ± 0.06 | 7% slower | 1101.43 KB | 1171.09 KB | 1.06 |
| Optimized hierarchy presenter | 2.542 ms | 2.681 ms | 1.06 ± 0.06 | 6% slower | 1060.76 KB | 1127.63 KB | 1.06 |
| Direct retained hierarchy | 2.637 ms | 2.635 ms | 1.01 ± 0.08 | parity | 1060.76 KB | 1128.14 KB | 1.06 |
| Direct hierarchy + built-in drawn | 3.733 ms | 6.398 ms | 1.72 ± 0.08 | 72% slower | 1747.91 KB | 3062.38 KB | 1.75 |
| Direct hierarchy + custom Skia | 1.049 ms | 1.166 ms | 1.11 ± 0.07 | 11% slower | 338.32 KB | 377.60 KB | 1.12 |

The optimized retained-theme regression that motivated this run is no longer
pathological. The final BDN ratio is 1.07 ± 0.06, versus the earlier stopwatch
observation of 3.77×. A stable BDN probe before the final recycle changes was
1.20× time and 1.21× allocation; the final result is 1.07× time and 1.06×
allocation.

## Original hierarchy-model suite

configuration. It separates hierarchy mutation from DataGrid realization and
layout.

|---|---|---:|---:|
| `CollapseAll` | 149,792-node optimized sample | 4.184 ms | 4.39 KB |
